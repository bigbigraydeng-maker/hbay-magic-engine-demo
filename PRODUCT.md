# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS served from GitHub Pages; no build step (existing repository).

## Users

New Zealand grocery shoppers choosing everyday bottled water, mostly on a phone, often at home or near a store. Secondary: retail, hospitality and export buyers looking for a supplier (served by the Partner and Trade pages, not the home page).

## Product Purpose

HBay Water is natural artesian water bottled in Hastings, Hawke's Bay, by New Zealand Miracle Water Ltd. The site is a design demo prepared by Magic Engine for client review. The home page must make HBay recognisable as a brand and send shoppers to a store that stocks it.

## Positioning

Artesian water from a named New Zealand region, sold in mainstream NZ supermarkets in a full range of sizes, from 330 mL bottles to 16 L containers and bag-in-box.

## Brand commitments

- Slogan (confirmed): "More Natural Better Living".
- Logo and brand blue #1E95D4 from `assets/brand/hbay-logo.svg`; pack label uses a pink-red "PURE NEW ZEALAND" band.
- PM direction (2026-10-15): brand leads, factory content stays off the home page; benchmark against Antipodes, Waiakea and Mountain Valley: one image style, no text baked into images, few sections.
- English only, aimed at mainstream New Zealand.

## Evidence and claims

- Verified retailers: PAK'nSAVE and New World product search; Four Square disclosed by the brand.
- Sizes from the client's site: 330 mL, 500 mL, 600 mL, 1.5 L bottles; 6 L, 10 L, 16 L containers; 7 L bag; 10 L bag-in-box.
- "250,000 years" refers to the formation of the Heretaunga Plains (1997 groundwater study), never the age of the water.
- Do not use: "government-backed", "top 3", "since 2015" (unverified or conflicting).
- Lifestyle and source imagery is AI-generated and must be labelled as such.
