Scope: design/index.html (home). Mode: Persuade. Audience: NZ grocery shoppers on a phone. Action: find a store that stocks HBay. Proof: the real range, the real retailers, a sourced geology story.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the HBay pack, opened out: the label's blue field and its pink-red "PURE NEW ZEALAND" band become the page's structure. Refuses the category default of a split hero over a row of lifestyle cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground and HBay blue fields (#1E95D4 for colour, #0B5F8F for text-bearing fields), deep navy ink #0A2540, one pink-red band #D93A62 used only as the label band. Figtree throughout, heavy display weight, tight tracking. Photography in one style: bright, clean, daylight. No cards, no kickers, no text baked into images.

STORY: The visitor sees the bottle in its landscape, reads where it comes from, sees every size, learns why artesian water is different, and taps through to a retailer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photograph of the bottle by the lake, bottle right of centre. Headline "Artesian water from Hawke's Bay." large at left over a navy scrim, slogan under it, primary button "Find HBay in store" and a quiet "See the range" link. The pink-red label band runs across the bottom edge of the hero.

FORM: user-pinned five-section plan (hero, range, source, where to buy, trade line); code-led; no seed key (pinned direction, concept-seed not run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
