Scope: design/index.html (home). Mode: Persuade. Audience: New Zealand shoppers who want everyday quality: pure NZ water that feels like a better life without a luxury price. Action: find a store that stocks HBay. Proof: the real range, real retailers, sourced water facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HBay is the water on the table at a Hawke's Bay long lunch. The page is a cellar-door tasting card: the region's wine-country hospitality, applied to water. Refuses the category default of a blue splash and snowy peaks, and the supermarket-shelf page that came before.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white table linen ground (#F7F8F6, not cream), deep navy ink #0E2233, HBay blue #1E95D4 only for links and the logo, warm light carried by photography alone. Bodoni Moda display (a wine-label Didone; reason: tasting cards and estate labels in this region are set in high-contrast serifs), Hanken Grotesk text. Hairline rules like a printed tasting sheet; label/value rows instead of cards. No kickers, no text baked into images.

STORY: The visitor sees a set table among the vines, reads where HBay comes from, reads its tasting notes like a wine, sees it at everyday moments, picks a size from "the list", and finds a store.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed long-lunch photograph (bottles on a set table, vines and water beyond). Headline in Bodoni Moda over a soft lower scrim, lower left: "Bottled in Hawke's Bay. Poured at the long lunch." Slogan "More Natural Better Living" beneath, then "Find HBay in store" button and a "Read the tasting notes" link.
