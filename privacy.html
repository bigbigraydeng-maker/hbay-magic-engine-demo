Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Who collects the information

This website is operated by New Zealand Miracle Water Ltd, the producer of HBay Natural Artesian Water. Personal information is handled in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Information collected

  • Email address, when subscribing to the newsletter.
  • Name, company, email address, phone number and message, when sending a trade or partnership enquiry.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited, collected by the web server and analytics tools.

How the information is used

  • To send newsletters, product updates and stockist news to subscribers.
  • To respond to enquiries and manage trade, distribution and partnership relationships.
  • To maintain and improve the website.

Personal information is not sold. It is shared only with service providers that host the website or send email on the company’s behalf, or where disclosure is required by law.

Newsletter

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribe requests can also be sent to admin@nzmiracle.com.

Storage and security

Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, and reasonable safeguards are in place to protect it from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Cookies

The website may use cookies to remember preferences and measure site usage. Cookies can be disabled in browser settings; some features may then not work as intended.

Access and correction

Under the Privacy Act 2020, any person may request access to, or correction of, the personal information held about them. Requests can be made using the contact details below.
