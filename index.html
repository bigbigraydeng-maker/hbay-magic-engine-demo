OUR SOURCE

Natural Artesian Water from Hawke’s Bay

Rivers and changing coastlines laid down layers of gravel and fine sediment across Hawke’s Bay. Where groundwater is confined beneath them, natural pressure makes it rise. HBay is that artesian water, bottled in Hastings, close to the source.

SOURCED FROM HAWKE’S BAY

On the east coast of New Zealand’s North Island, rivers carried gravel down from the ranges for thousands of years. Beneath the Heretaunga Plains that gravel now holds the water HBay is drawn from.
