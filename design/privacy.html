What we collect

When you use an enquiry form on this site we ask for your name, email and message, and sometimes your company, phone number, market and products of interest. We only ask for what we need to reply.

How we use it

New Zealand Miracle Water Ltd uses these details to reply to your enquiry and to discuss working together. We do not sell your details.

How the forms work

In this design demo, sending a form opens a pre-filled email to our team from your own email app. The demo site itself does not store what you type.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Write to admin@nzmiracle.com.
