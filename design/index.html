Beneath the Heretaunga Plains.

Rivers and changing coastlines laid down layer after layer of gravel and fine sediment across Hawke’s Bay. Water held deep in those layers rises under its own pressure. That is what makes it artesian.

250,000 years of layers, by the regional geology.

The figure describes how the plains formed, not the age of the water. Source: Heretaunga Plains Groundwater Study, 1997.
